Satoru Gojo

Satoru Gojo is the strongest special-grade jujutsu sorcerer from Jujutsu Kaisen. His abilities rely on combining his inherited spatial technique with god-tier sensory perception.

Core Techniques

  • Cursed Technique Lapse: Blue: Creates a magnetic-like gravitational pull by generating negative distance in space, crushing or dragging everything toward a central point.
  • Cursed Technique Reversal: Red: The inverse of Blue that uses positive cursed energy to create a massive repulsive force, blasting away everything in its path with double the power of Blue.
  • Hollow Technique: Purple: A combination of Blue and Red that merges attraction and repulsion into an imaginary mass, erasing anything in its trajectory.
  • Black Flash: A distortion in space caused by applying cursed energy within a 0.000001-second window of a physical hit, elevating his strike to 120% power.

Domain Expansion & Anti-Domain

  • Unlimited Void (Immeasurable Void): His domain expansion creates a psychic space that floods the target's brain with an infinite amount of sensory information and knowledge, paralyzing them completely.
  • Simple Domain & Falling Blossom Emotion: Anti-domain techniques used to neutralize or deflect guaranteed-hit attacks from enemy domains.

Movement & Support

  • Teleportation: Compresses space between himself and a destination to move instantaneously (or fold a path instantly).
  • Flight / Levitation: Manipulates spatial coordinates to float or travel through the air at high speeds.
  • Reversed Cursed Technique (RCT): Channels positive energy to rapidly heal his own body or refuel his brain against burnout.

Ability Activated

Infinite power unleashed across space.
